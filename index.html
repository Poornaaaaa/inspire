The Executive Vision

A Stepping Stone for Tomorrow's Tech Leaders

Bridging the gap between academic classroom learning and high-stakes corporate challenges.

INSPIRE is an intra-department fest organized by the Department of Computer Science in collaboration with the IT Club and dedicated third-year students at St. Claret College.

The fest is designed especially for first- and second-year BCA students, providing them with a dedicated platform to enhance their decision-making skills, teamwork, creativity, problem-solving ability, and IT proficiency through a series of engaging competitions and activities.

"The objective of INSPIRE is not only to foster technical knowledge and practical exposure, but also to build confidence, leadership qualities, and a spirit of healthy competition among participants."

Events are thoughtfully curated to strike a balance between academic excellence and fun learning, ensuring students grow both personally and professionally. By participating, young learners prepare themselves for intercollegiate fests and real-world industrial challenges.
